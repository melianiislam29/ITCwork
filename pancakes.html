Moon Pancakes

the easiest breakfast in the galaxy !

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 spoon of sugar

How to make them

  • 1.mix all the ingredients in a bowl.
  • 2.poor a little mix into a warm pan.
  • 3.cook both sides until golden.
  • 4.add you favorite topping!
chef's tip :

add a banana for extra happiness.🍌

Enjoy your pancakes!
